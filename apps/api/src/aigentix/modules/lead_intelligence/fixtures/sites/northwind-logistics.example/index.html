Northwind Logistics: freight forwarding that keeps your goods moving

Northwind Logistics is a family-run freight forwarder based in Rotterdam, moving goods for small and mid-sized importers and exporters across Europe, North America and Asia. We combine air, sea and road freight with hands-on customs support so you do not have to manage five different suppliers.

What we do

  • Sea freight (FCL and LCL)
  • Air freight and express
  • Road freight across the EU
  • Customs clearance and documentation
