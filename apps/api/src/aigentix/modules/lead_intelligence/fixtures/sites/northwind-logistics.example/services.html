Our services

Sea freight

Full container (FCL) and shared container (LCL) services from all major European ports with weekly consolidations to the US East Coast, Shanghai and Singapore.

Air freight

Door-to-door air freight with express options for urgent or high-value cargo. Same-day quotes on request.

Road freight

Daily groupage and full-truck-load services across Benelux, Germany, France and Poland.

Customs clearance

Import and export declarations, tariff classification, duty deferment and certificates of origin. Exporters can download our customs declaration template (PDF).

Warehousing

Bonded and non-bonded storage near the port of Rotterdam with pick-and-pack for online retailers.
