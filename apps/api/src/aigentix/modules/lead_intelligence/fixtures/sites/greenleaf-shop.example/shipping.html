Shipping and returns

Delivery

Orders ship Monday to Thursday in recyclable packaging. Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days; express is next-day. You receive a tracking link by email.

Order status

Want to know where your order is? Email support with your order number. We check each order manually against the carrier site, and we currently answer around 60 order-status questions a day.

Returns process

  1. Contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and photos.
  2. We confirm whether a refund or replacement applies.
  3. Return label is emailed to you; refunds are processed within 5 working days of receipt.

Plants damaged in transit are replaced free of charge. See the FAQ or support.
