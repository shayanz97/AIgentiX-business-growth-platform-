About Northwind Logistics

Founded in 2009 by two former dock supervisors, Northwind Logistics has grown from a two-person office into a team of 45 people across Rotterdam, Hamburg and Chicago. We handle roughly 2,400 shipments a year for about 380 active customers, most of them small and mid-sized manufacturers, wholesalers and online retailers.

Our promise is simple: one named contact, honest transit times and no surprise charges. We are a certified customs broker and an authorised economic operator (AEO).

Our values

Clear communication, practical solutions and long-term relationships. Many of our customers have been shipping with us for over a decade.
