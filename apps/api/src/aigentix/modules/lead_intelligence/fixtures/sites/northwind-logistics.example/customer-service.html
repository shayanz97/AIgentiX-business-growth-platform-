Customer service

Our customer service team is the heart of Northwind Logistics. On a typical day we handle 300+ inquiries by email, phone and web form, mostly asking where a shipment is, which documents are missing and when an invoice will be issued.

Opening hours

Monday to Friday, 07:30 to 18:00 CET. Out-of-hours emergencies: +1-555-0101.

Inquiry types we handle

  • Shipment status and ETA updates
  • Customs documents and holds
  • Booking changes and cancellations
  • Invoice and payment questions
  • Damage and claims

How to reach us

Email service@northwind-logistics.example, call +1-555-0100, or use the shipment inquiry form. Answers to common questions are on the FAQ page.

Average first response time is currently about 3 hours; most replies are written manually from our shipping system and mailbox.
