Frequently asked questions

How do I get a freight quote?

Send us origin, destination, cargo type, weight and dimensions through the contact form. We reply with a quote within four business hours.

How long does sea freight take?

Rotterdam to New York is typically 12 to 16 days port to port. Asia routes take 28 to 38 days.

Can you handle customs clearance for me?

Yes. We are a licensed customs broker and can clear imports and exports in the EU and the US.

Which documents are required for export?

Commercial invoice, packing list and, depending on goods, certificate of origin and export licence. Our customs declaration template is available as a PDF download.

How do I track my shipment?

Use the shipment inquiry form with your booking reference. A coordinator will send you the status.

Do you offer cargo insurance?

Yes, all-risk cover from 0.3 percent of the cargo value.

What if my cargo is damaged?

Report it within 3 days and send photos. We will open a claim with the carrier.

Do you store goods?

Yes, bonded and non-bonded warehousing in Rotterdam.

What are your payment terms?

Standard terms are 30 days from invoice date for approved accounts.

Can you ship dangerous goods?

Yes, ADR and IMDG class goods on request, with extra lead time.

Do you work with small shippers?

Absolutely. Many customers ship fewer than ten consignments a year.

How do I change a booking?

Contact customer service with your reference at least 48 hours before cut-off.
