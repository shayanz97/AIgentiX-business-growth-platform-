About Fjord Accounting

Fjord Accounting was established in 2004 and is owned by its partners, all of whom are certified accountants. We believe good accounting is a routine, not a once-a-year panic. Our clients range from sole traders to companies with 50 employees.

Our team works from a single office and remotely. We are proud of our low staff turnover and our tidy folders.
