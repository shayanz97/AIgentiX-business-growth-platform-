How clients send us documents

Each client has a secure shared folder. Every month, clients upload receipts, supplier invoices, bank statements and payroll changes. Many clients still email scans or photos of paper receipts; our bookkeepers then rename, sort and key the data into the accounting system by hand.

Monthly checklist

  1. Upload all receipts and invoices by the 5th of the month.
  2. Send timesheets or payroll changes by the 20th.
  3. Reply to our query list within three working days.

Common issues

Missing receipts, unreadable photos and duplicate invoices are the most common causes of delay. We chase clients by email and phone every month.
